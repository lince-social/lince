# World and production ideas

These ideas remain unscheduled. They can be considered with Interfaceless or Ergon in [Lince.lingua](../Lince.lingua), rather than expanding ordinary interface work automatically.

A Map could show Records and people whose locations they choose to share. It could grow to include streets, terrain, routes, Needs and Contributions. Geographic height and a height representing data have different meanings and should remain distinguishable.

World scenes could combine Earth, authored places, captured spaces and imagined futures. Keep exact private placement separate from a coarser public location. Maps need attribution, licensing, bounded downloads and useful offline behavior. Bevy is the presentation base; no hosted map provider is required by the idea.

Stable location and shared coordinate frames matter from globe scale down to a desk or Castle. Keep source geometry and capture provenance, with derived meshes and levels of detail that can be rebuilt. Observed, desired and simulated worlds should be comparable without overwriting one another. Model-generated interpretations are proposals for human review before they become Actions, Needs or Transfers.

Ergon concerns coordinating production for Needs. Possible views include stock, orders, production dependencies, distribution and the effect of changing demand or supply. A disruption at a mineral supplier could reveal consequences further along the chain. Existing Records, quantities, relations and Transfer views may provide much of this; a new Castle should answer an actual missing need.

A public shop is another possibility: an order could create and assign production Needs. Payments and public authority need their own design before that becomes a working flow.

These ideas are referenced once from [the interface draft](plans/interface.md#ideas-to-keep-separate). They carry no completion promises here.
