# CEF — the final v1 lane

CEF and browser-dependent Sands remain in the codebase but are disabled during Part A and the following native work. This lane is scheduled at the end of v1, after the native Box and its other planned native features. It is not a C5 or Calendar dependency. The [delivery/build rule](../build.md) defines the optional feature boundary; the existing [HTML/Website contract](../html-and-websites.md) retains its authority and isolation requirements.

The old joined reports prove their historical revisions only. No promise is made that dormant CEF code will remain integrated without rework. Re-enabling it requires the following work and fresh proof; builders must not run it opportunistically during Part A to keep those old reports green.

The sweep moved the five specialized browser roots and the CEF-specific parts of Part A's former checks here. Each moved requirement has a task below; shared security/package detail stays in its existing subject document, not another competing checklist. The later native and CEF deliveries use the same definitions and domain semantics.
