# Fiote voice workflow — remaining acceptance

The intended workflow is: install/import the supported English transcription model, configure a microphone and three wake examples, enable listening, say “Hey Fiote” and a request, reach the appropriate Fiote, and receive a written/spoken reply or an action. Follow-ups stay in that conversation without another wake phrase. Closing, unrelated or uncertain speech, ninety idle seconds, End, or Stop ends the binding; Stop keeps capture off.

Testing and fixes are authorized. The owner explicitly chose simulated recordings. Human voice accuracy and room audibility are optional later validation, not prerequisites for this simulation. Native provider connections remain generic; ACP, external agents and Node.js are optional.

The simulated source and locally optimized default UI/media workflows now pass, including real subscription replies, spoken output, contextual follow-ups, natural ending and Stop. The optimized executable also opened a real Wayland window and passed its Laboratory Hello. Remaining acceptance concerns the exact published/Instinct-enabled release and the performance coverage identified below. Local semantic-model quality remains deferred.
