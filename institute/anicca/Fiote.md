## Intended configurable flow

Phonon turns speech into a transcript. By default that text goes to the automatic parent/intake Fiote, selected by most direct children and oldest on ties; that Fiote may answer, act or delegate according to its configuration. An explicit recipient overrides automatic selection. With local destination routing enabled and a supported model installed, a Jev-like classifier such as Laya receives the transcript and candidate Fiote descriptions, then selects a recipient. Failure, uncertainty or an unavailable router falls back to the default Fiote. Follow-ups remain in the selected Fiote's conversation.

This connection and its native controls already exist. Local choices include Laya, English NLI and Qwen; importing arbitrary weights does not add support for an unknown model architecture. Choosing a destination and deciding whether speech continues a conversation are separate decisions. Follow-up classification currently uses the selected Fiote's configured model through a tool-free contextual check unless a separately validated local admission model is available. Neither a Jev nor validation of its accuracy is required to use Phonon with the default Fiote.
