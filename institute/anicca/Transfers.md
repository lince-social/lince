## Reuse boundaries and fixed decisions

- Reuse [combined trust evaluation](../../crates/engine/src/trust_analysis/remote/merge.rs), [query arithmetic](../../crates/nucleus/src/trust_analysis/query.rs), [Trust Castle](../../crates/interface/src/native/trust_castle.rs), [signed public terms](../../crates/nucleus/src/transfer/public_history.rs) and existing receipt/contact/claim/recipe services. Trust is visible experience analyzed under the reader's policy, not money, a global score or proof of physical delivery.
- Keep exact quantities grouped by immutable Vocabulary/Concept/unit versions. Separate giver claims, receiver confirmations, settlement and private effects. The strongest metric requires the receiver's Need, exact meanings, confirmation and attributable settlement. Deduplicate original occurrence/outcome identities, not merely file hashes. Copies and observers earn no contribution credit.
- Preserve independent endpoint, issuer, counterparty and path-intermediary filters. Include conditions combine with OR, criteria within a condition with AND, exclusions win. Lower Organ contact values mean closer, not more reliable. Known identity does not imply relied-on testimony. Person permission does not imply Organ permission.
- Retain configurable evidence weights 1 / 0.5 / 0.1 for Need-linked receiver / receiver-only / giver-only evidence. Direct-contact weight is 1 and halves each further hop. Path depth defaults to 3, maximum 6. Caps default off and affect indices only. Raw amounts remain visible separately.
- Preserve remote limits: 1024 identities, 5000 events, 8 MiB aggregate, four concurrent operations, 15-second deadline. Profile opening and local verification send no discovery request. Reader collection requires reviewed Start/Refresh. A changed claim subject, meaning/unit, period, role, basis or audience needs fresh review. Missing support is unknown/partial, not a fraud verdict.
- Reuse [Record location](../../crates/engine/src/location.rs), [shared controls](../../crates/interface/src/location.rs), #address and visibility policies. Saved places, approximate areas and live sharing already exist; do not add them again or duplicate the other agent's address work. A public #x transfer can privately affect #y Records without exposing that mapping. Preserve the existing limit of 32 private effect targets.
- Live sharing remains private by default, explicitly sourced and independently approved per participant: default one hour, maximum one day, maximum fresh-fix age 60 seconds. Accuracy and intentionally shared radius are separate. No map, agreement or settlement grants location access.
- Reuse normal Transfer revisions, agreements, reservations, dependencies, Karma, signed announcements and simulations. Add no second ledger, automation engine or Transport permission model.
- Keep [Galileo/Bevy maps](../../crates/map/src/lib.rs), Natural Earth overview, Shortbread detail without buildings, local directories/PMTiles, configured optional HTTP and theme tokens. Data zoom 0–14; display overzoom to 20 creates no detail. Maps remain optional for every Transfer. Do not reopen stack selection.
