- [ ] Define the expected behavior and smallest decisive checks, implement the slice, inspect its native result where applicable, run the affected checks, repair observed failures and record the outcome before choosing the next slice. - [ ] Allow a temporary earlier-stage regression during an explicit implementation cycle. Record the affected workflow/contract, reproducer, responsible current task and repair point immediately; prioritize repair at the next safe step. Keep unrelated usable work available. - [ ] When a verified stage regresses, preserve its historical proof but mark its current verification `Repair required` in the registry and handoff; dependent advance gates stop accepting that proof. Restore `Verified` only after the relevant repair checks and composed workflow pass. Pending stages remain pending; a plan edit never supplies runtime evidence.

- [ ] Maintain a small relevant regression set from previously verified workflow contracts. Run affected unit/contract/integration checks during the cycle, the relevant native/composed workflow at its integration boundary and the stage's full applicable gate before advancement. - [ ] Let each cycle retire a concrete pending requirement, make a measured improvement or close a recorded defect/decision. Preserve useful evidence and reuse a proven implementation while its requirements hold; avoid reopening resolved choices without new information.

## Workflow contracts and balanced acceptance

- [ ] Treat this guide as context routing and each stage as the detailed specification for its pending work. Retain architecture, small consequential decisions, ownership, data structures, schemas/interfaces, algorithms, stack constraints, examples and failure paths at the owning task. Distinguish an illustrative sketch from a selected production interface and record how unresolved choices will be decided. Detail has no arbitrary word cap while it prevents a real implementation ambiguity; do not copy every past or future subsystem into the current context.

- [ ] Keep the stage's opening milestones and `NN.Wxx` rows as persistent capability contracts. Classify each workflow as a **built-in capability** delivered by that stage, an **example recipe** composing existing primitives with a small saved arrangement/asset/configuration, or a named combination of both. State any adapter actually required. A recipe uses the shared edit/save/query/authority/demand path; a new independent product implementation requires an explicit scope decision.

- [ ] Preserve every required example as evidence while keeping its data outside generic engine logic. The solar/ball journey, invented scene, room/castle, CAD bracket, articulated body/mechanism and laboratory specimen prove supported operations and composition; their names, dimensions, appearance and geographic hierarchy do not become mandatory engine categories. An optional idea does not silently become a delivery gate.

- [ ] For every retained workflow, capture fixture/source/build/profile, starting state, native actions, expected observable result, required primitive combination, hard assertions, qualitative criteria, quantitative thresholds and failure/recovery cases. Map every required milestone to at least one row; a short matrix row points to detailed owned requirements instead of weakening them. In completed stages retain a separate compact paragraph for each workflow, so earlier intended uses remain recoverable.

- [ ] Separate **hard requirements** from **soft quality criteria**, and separate both from measurements still being explored. Hard requirements include supported operations, identity/units/authority, integrity/recovery and adopted numeric error/resource/latency bounds. Soft criteria include low-poly appeal, readable content, predictable controls, useful feedback and understandable repair. Give soft criteria a task-specific rubric, views/actions and recorded pass/fail observations; they remain acceptance obligations rather than optional polish. A reference screenshot supports review but does not replace interaction or structural assertions.

- [ ] Adopt actual workload sizes, thresholds, measurement points and hardware from stage 01 and the owning fixture/profile before implementing the tested slice. Proposed numbers stay identified until selected; unspecified values remain pending with an owner. Record absolute results and applicable baseline deltas, including transient peaks, cold/warm/offline conditions, percentile sampling and relevant accuracy. A faster result with weakened detail, precision, coverage or permissions is a changed experiment. Change a governing requirement/profile only through the existing explicit scope-review process; ordinary implementation choices reuse existing authorization.

- [ ] Combine meaningful test levels: unit/property/metamorphic checks for local invariants; integration and controlled fault/retry tests for accepted operations, persistence, jobs and authority; native end-to-end interaction simulation for the real widgets/input/focus/command path; and bounded performance/resource runs plus qualitative review of the actual experience. Choose levels that expose the changed risk. Stage 01 validates planning/baseline artifacts, and future solvers run only in their authorized stages. A rendered square count, a compiled example or a mocked service alone cannot complete a composed workflow.

- [ ] Drive native interaction simulations through supported pointer/keyboard/tool inputs and the actual services, then assert selected identities, accepted revisions/receipts, visible state and saved/reopened results as applicable. Use controlled clocks/completions for races and keep timing benchmarks separate from instrumentation-heavy tests. Record unsupported automation and a reproducible manual interaction recipe where necessary; a skipped required interaction keeps its evidence pending. Keep tests proportional to the feature and failure boundary.

- [ ] For each newly composed experience, retain at least one relevant already verified user workflow and identify the earlier contracts that could break. Test interoperability through the actual public/module boundary rather than requiring the same internal implementation. Stable capabilities may survive a justified rewrite; tests should not freeze incidental helper names, private struct layout or arbitrary screenshot pixels.

- [ ] Keep a traceable chain for each required outcome: `NN.Mxx → NN.Wxx → owning NN.Txx/requirement → acceptance replay → observed evidence → retained handoff`. The stage-local `NN.Pxx` proof recipes below make this chain concrete; use them within the written task order, not as a competing implementation sequence. One proof can cover several requirements, but explicitly list its coverage. A requirement without evidence stays open; an example recipe cannot conceal an unimplemented primitive or adapter.

- [ ] Before the affected implementation starts, fill each proof recipe's workload dimensions, adopted limits and observables from the named fixture/profile. Separate input size, active set and addressable extent; separate unique source assets from occurrences, participants and views. Name the unit, sampling interval/count, start/stop event and pass condition for each measurement. Unknown, unsupported, not measured, failed and passed are different states; an empty field or an unavailable GPU counter is never zero or a pass.

- [ ] Make checks capable of detecting a plausible defect: derive expected quantities/identities from an independently specified fixture, reference calculation or invariant rather than echoing the implementation's output. Use controlled completions/clocks for reproducible acceptance races, and real elapsed time for performance. Record seeds/failure points where relevant. A single interaction sample is a sample, not a meaningful p95; select enough samples and report their count without making ordinary correctness tests depend on benchmark timing.

- [ ] Define qualitative pass observations before inspecting the result: which actions/viewpoints expose the concern, what the user must distinguish or accomplish, and which visible/control failures require repair. Record the actual observation/reviewer and native evidence. Automation may establish interaction/focus/identity invariants; an unreviewed screenshot cannot establish attractive low-poly form, comprehensible repair or useful character motion.

- [ ] Preserve one distinct compact paragraph per retained workflow, keyed by `NN.Wxx` and its original milestone IDs. State what the user can still do, which primitives/short recipe provide it, the relevant hard bounds and quality criteria, and links to runnable evidence. The implementation paragraphs' length target does not justify dropping a workflow or its governing conditions.

- [ ] Keep authoritative schemas/contracts, fixture definitions and consequential decisions accessible at stable links in code, tests, the handoff or a compact retained section. Redirect incoming links before removing obsolete planning detail. Delete superseded pseudocode, duplicate research and completed step-by-step instructions once the real implementation/evidence takes their place; keep every unfinished, blocked and deferred requirement detailed.

- [ ] Preserve measured profile/version/source and proof links separately from instructions. The completion handoff records implementation rationale, concise relevant alternatives, accepted interfaces/ownership, workflow evidence, hard/soft results, unresolved scope, current regressions and what the next stage needs. A completed paragraph is a capability contract with traceable evidence, not a success story.

- [ ] When an earlier contract changes, update its retained workflow paragraphs and factual handoff to the new verified implementation without erasing the original capability requirement. Repair affected later references/tests and return to the earliest remaining task. A future agent should recover the next task from the guide, required handoffs and current stage without reconstructing the conversation.

- [ ] Prune by verified requirement rather than by checked line count. If a paragraph mixes delivered and pending work, split it and retain the unfinished portion with its original ID/owner. Preserve links/anchors used by later stages; keep an alias or update every incoming reference before removing a heading. Never shorten a requirement by deleting its limits, failure behavior, native interaction or workflow composition.

- [ ] Make each retained workflow paragraph answer: **what still works; built-in capability or short recipe; which source/command/representation boundaries implement it; hard limits and quality obligations; where to rerun its proof**. Keep implementation/rationale to one or two paragraphs overall and useful rejected alternatives to a sentence; keep separate workflow paragraphs even when the stage has many. Leave full pending architecture, schemas, algorithms and examples available until their work is verified.

- [ ] End each working cycle with the earliest remaining task, delivered subset, decisive result and next action/repair in the existing card. Do not create another plan or re-investigate settled alternatives merely because context was lost. Repeated failure warrants a changed hypothesis or targeted architecture decision; repeating a passing unchanged check warrants no new completion claim.

## Shared decisions to preserve

- [ ] Keep the kernel domain-neutral: identities/occurrences/revisions; units/frames/clocks; supports/selections; typed values/evidence/unknowns; relations/constraints; operators/dependencies; purpose-specific representations; explicit runtime state. Workflows reuse these primitives rather than owning parallel model/save/query/undo systems.

- [ ] Keep ownership small: proposed pure `workspace_model` may depend on pure nucleus types; nucleus never imports it. Engine/store own authorized acceptance/persistence, native interface owns rendering/tools, map supplies geographic data, transport/Cell/Organ own scoped delivery, Protein retains semantic/source sessions, and specialist workers appear only when a stage needs them. Keep scientific kernels out of ordinary viewers.

- [ ] Treat Fiote as an Actor with the Agent role, acting within delegated scope through the same authorized queries, typed commands, validation, receipts, cancellation and undo as native controls. Use [stage 02's Agent contract](02-stage.md#agent-actors-and-delegated-workspace-operations), [stage 04's bounded context](04-stage.md#structured-context-for-workspace-actors) and [stage 07's native authoring workflow](07-stage.md#native-scene-authoring-by-an-agent). Keep manual use complete when the provider is absent.

- [ ] Preserve one content/source identity across appropriate spatial, flat and HUD presentations, with view-local focus/tool state, independent anchors and aggregate demand budgets. Keep canonical placement and underlying Record/model state coherent; see [stage 03](03-stage.md#content-and-presentation-identity). Use existing compositor/application adapters for supported external content.

- [ ] Derive linked work from explicit model selections, revisioned quantities and declared recipes. Update unaccepted derived drafts live while preserving manual overrides; reconcile accepted assignments, commitments and Transfers through their authoritative operations. [Stage 11](11-stage.md#revisioned-takeoff-for-linked-work) supplies inputs and [stage 13](13-stage.md#live-derived-work-and-reconciliation) owns this workflow.

- [ ] Choose architecture for the concrete software capabilities in this sequence. Hardware and Geist/world-model research add zero deliverables, dependencies or acceptance gates to Workspace. Reuse the already needed model/view/input/observation/artifact boundaries where useful; add no speculative adapter framework, reserved schema or test obligation solely for those future directions. Native operations, reusable assets and imports provide Fiote's initial authoring path. Real equipment validation remains in Ailuros; preserve the software's existing calibration/output behavior where touched.

- [ ] Make scale scene-independent. The ball/Earth/solar route and independent invented scene are required examples, never fixed engine endpoints/hierarchy/zoom bands. Preserve local physical dimensions, exact authored distinctions, navigation up distinct from gravity, and sparse active cost independent of empty extent.

- [ ] Select Orbit/Fly/Walk explicitly; preserve supported pose/focus and horizon/street/interior use. Ground has no local relief and absent building/interior geometry stays absent unless supplied/authored. The globe remains curved; blank roots need no Earth. Map/overview proxies and street geometry identify the same user-authored place.

- [ ] Release unneeded RAM/GPU after bounded demand/fences; retain durable edits, bounded compressed disk cache and explicit offline packs. Fetch configured detail on demand online within persistent budgets; full workspace-offline starts no remote/source/heartbeat/reconnect request.

- [ ] Automatically apply ready applicable editor output to the captured selected local scope on exit; preserve pending/invalid/stale state and previous accepted output otherwise. Keep ordinary multi-copy editing simple, source lineage retained and deliberate shared-definition updates explicit. Exceptional unsupported continuation retains last usable output and pauses only affected evolution before a visible refresh/restart.

- [ ] Preserve scoped identity/rights: private invites plus optional explicit public visitors, identified authorized editors, optional nonphysical avatars/cursors and designated runtime authority. Entry cost/preflight is bounded and continuing limits are enforced. No invite lends another Organ's login, private source, execution, desktop/pixels/input or worker budget.

- [ ] Keep permitted cached viewing and drafts after host loss; revalidate/reconcile with the same authority and recorded request outcomes. Do not automatically replay mutations, elect a physics writer or donate participant compute. Distinguish accepted geometry, observations, operational facts and conditional results.

- [ ] Preserve direct empirical material choice without microscopic setup, independent specimen/laboratory roots and amount-versus-sample separation. Structural editing is stage 12; new property derivation, experiments and physical behavior wait for the deferred tail. Atoms/proteins do not replicate by moles to simulate a bulk object.

- [ ] Model characters, animals, robots and mechanisms through the same articulated relations, reference configurations, pose coordinates, deformation bindings, attachments and motion/controller operators. Stage 05 supplies lightweight first/third-person navigation, stage 07 customizes supplied appearance, and [stage 11](11-stage.md#articulated-models-rigs-and-anatomy) delivers shared rig/pose/motion authoring and optional anatomical references. Preserve one model/command/save/query/undo path; do not make a humanoid skeleton or anatomy pack a kernel requirement.

- [ ] Control structural coverage, visible detail and calculated behavior independently. Detailed anatomical structures can unload after editing while a simpler surface, rig and support proxy retain authored behavior. Pose/animation authoring is core; additional force-driven joints, physical cloth/hair, active muscles, tissue mechanics and biomechanical predictions remain deferred. Fiote receives permitted named structures, captured poses and attributed annotations; clinical diagnosis is not a core capability or acceptance gate.
