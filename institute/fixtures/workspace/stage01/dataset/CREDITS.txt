Made with Natural Earth.
Source: Natural Earth 1:110m coastline, version 4.1.0.
Upstream commit: 693f11422f4e08d2da4566b854dda53eb7c39fb3.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/110m-coastline/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
The upstream vector data is in the public domain.
This fixture retains the upstream GeoJSON bytes, including its longitude/latitude coordinates.
This is schematic global context, not local survey or terrain-elevation data.

Synthetic geometry and the derived rigid bandana are original Lince fixture assets under MIT.
Fiote lynx character
Original stylized Canada lynx artwork, mesh, rig, and animation created for Lince.
Author: Lince contributors.
License: MIT; see LICENSE in this directory.
The connected coat, blended skin weights, paw IK, and neutral 128px fur texture are original Lince assets.
Motion seams on ears, brows, paws, and tail are colored by Lince's theme tokens at runtime.
No third-party meshes, textures, fonts, or artwork are embedded.
Native glTF source data under source/ retains the original mesh, skin, materials, texture, and baked animation clips.
cargo xtask maintenance fiote export packs source/fiote.gltf and source/fiote.bin into fiote.glb.
cargo xtask maintenance fiote validate checks the exported mesh, skin weights, animation samples, paws, and loop seams.
cargo xtask maintenance fiote render renders sit_idle to a transparent PNG using the Rust CPU renderer.
fiote.blend is retained as an archival authoring file and is not used by the native workflow.
fiote-seated.svg is independently maintained vector artwork of the same character.
