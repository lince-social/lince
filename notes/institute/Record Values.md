In Karma, a Record may have all it's properties used as Conditions, or Consequences.

As Conditions, you may grab the quantity of a Record with 'rq(Id)'. Example: 'rq2 + 1', which is then evaluated to number '5 +1' and the Condition turns out to be 6.

If you wanted to grab the 'head' of the Record you would write 'rh(Id)' like 'rh4'. For 'body' it would be 'rb(Id)'.
