Managing your progression through an imported Trail (see 'Alexandria- Information Trail' for the Trail shape itself).

Under the rebirth architecture (docs/fable-improvement.md):

- **Importing a Trail copies the shared subgraph** (records + links + concepts) into your Cell. Your copies keep concept identity — so the Trail stays connected to its source and to other Trails — but have independent quantities: your progress is yours. (This is the Record Sync idea: shared tree, personal numbers.)
- **Each step becomes a promise to yourself**: a delta with an optional window ("finish module 2 by March"). Sequenced steps are promises conditioned on the previous step being kept — the link graph (`@before`, `@requires`) gives the order, no extra ordering machinery.
- **Completing a step is a fact** (cause = you), so progress history lives in the Ledger: streaks, time-per-step, revisits, and reviews are all Protein queries, not bespoke tracking columns.
- **Karma keeps the pressure on**: the same counters used for habits keep the current step's Need active daily/weekly; a rule can advance the "current step" pointer (a record) when the completion fact lands.
- **Imagination projects the learning timeline**: at your current pace of completion facts, module 5 lands in April; change the practice frequency and watch the projection move.
- **A class or course is the same shape inside an Organ**: the teacher publishes the Trail; each student's progression facts (with due visibility) let the Organ see cohort progress — GdE's classes and sprints without any education-specific core feature.
